  artists:
  - name: Kurt Elling
    born: 1967
  prose: |-
    Kurt Elling is a highly acclaimed vocalist with a long list of awards, including multiple Grammy nominations and a 2009 win. He was voted Best Male Vocalist in the DownBeat Critics Poll for 15 years (2000-2013, 2018-2019). His style integrates scat, vocalese, improvised lyrics, and literary references, drawing comparisons to 1950s poet-comedian Lord Buckley.

- heading: Esperanza Spalding
  artists:
  - name: Esperanza Spalding
    born: 1984
  prose: |-
    Esperanza Spalding is a vocalist, bassist, composer, and a prodigious talent. She attended Berklee and became the youngest instructor in Berklee’s history at age 20. She’s won multiple Grammys, including a historic Best New Artist win in 2011 (the first jazz artist to do so).

    Drummer Terri Lyne Carrington once described her as having virtuosic bass technique, vocal acrobatics, challenging compositions, poetic lyrics, and commercial appeal.

- heading: Cécile McLorin Salvant
  artists:
  - name: Cécile McLorin Salvant
    born: 1989
  prose: |-
    Cécile McLorin Salvant, from Miami, won the 2010 Thelonious Monk competition and has dominated the DownBeat Critics Poll as Best Female Vocalist since 2014. She’s won multiple Grammys for Best Vocal Jazz Album. Salvant’s work blends historical reference and modern interpretation, with a brilliant command of phrasing and storytelling.
