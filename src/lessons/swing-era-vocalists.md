- heading: Ella Fitzgerald
  artists:
  - name: Ella Fitzgerald
    born: 1917
    died: 1996
  prose: |-
    Her nickname was the “First Lady of Song.”

    She was "discovered" when she won the Wednesday night amateur contest at the Apollo Theater in 1934 (a tradition carried on even today:
    <https://www.apollotheater.org/amateur-night/>
    ). Shortly thereafter, in the same year, she joined the Chick Webb Orchestra.

    When Webb died in 1939, Ella took over. The band became “Ella and Her Famous Orchestra.” It lasted until 1942, when Fitzgerald moved on to a solo-oriented career.

- heading: Frank Sinatra
  artists:
  - name: Frank Sinatra
    born: 1915
    died: 1998
  prose: |-
    Sometimes considered more of a pop singer than a jazz singer.

    Was a teen idol in the 40s. Career really picked up in the 50s and 60s.

    In the 50’s and 60’s gained a larger-than-life persona. Part of the “Rat Pack” with Sammy Davis, Jr., Dean Martin, etc.

    Strong influence on male jazz singers (and Seth McFarlane) today.

- heading: Nat “King” Cole
  artists:
  - name: Nat “King” Cole
    born: 1919
    died: 1965
  prose: |-
    Pioneer of the piano trio.

    First African-American person to have his own TV show: *The Nat King Cole Show* . It was a variety show on NBC that featured his playing and singing as well as many of the important jazz and pop artists of the day [(Bierman, p.184)](bibliography.html#bierman-2016)

    “A committed smoker – he believed it helped his voice – Cole died at age 45 from lung cancer” [(Bierman, p.184)](bibliography.html#bierman-2016)

    Sometimes accused of “selling out” because he sang pop songs. This is a criticism that would be levied against many jazz musicians, and indeed still frequently is!
