- heading: Bill Evans
  artists:
  - name: Bill Evans
    born: 1929
    died: 1980
  prose: |-
    William John Evans

    From an interview with Motian on how to pronounce his name: "Motion." I have been doing that for the last thirty years, but before that, I used to pronounce it "Motian" and people used to say "Motan" or "Moden." But when I was a kid, even as far back as kindergarten, people would say, "Paul Motion," so I said, "OK, from now on, if anybody asks me, I am going to say 'Paul Motion' and I am going to spell it the same way." So that is where it stands. Someone once said to me, "It doesn't make any difference, as long as they know who you're talking about."

    Pioneered the modern concept of a jazz piano trio with each instrumentalist being equally important.

    Had **four great trios** , defined mostly by the bass player:

    **#1**
    - Scott LaFaro (b) and Paul Motian (d) (1959 - 1961)

    **#2**
    - Chuck Israels (b) and Larry Bunker (d) (1961 - 1966)

    **#3**
    - Eddie Gomez (b) and Marty Morell (d), then Eliot Zigmund (d) (1966 - 1977)

    **#4**
    - Marc Johnson (b) and Joe LaBarbera (d) (1978 - 1980)

    1961 - Evans #3 in *Downbeat* magazine. (Oscar Peterson #1 - Thelonious Monk #2 - Brubeck)

    December 1968 *Downbeat* poll - Evans #2 (Herbie #1, Oscar Peterson #3)
