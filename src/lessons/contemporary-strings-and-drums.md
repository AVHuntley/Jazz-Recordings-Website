- heading: Kurt Rosenwinkel
  artists:
  - name: Kurt Rosenwinkel
    born: 1970
  prose: |-
    Kurt Rosenwinkel is one of the most influential guitarists of his generation. He often uses a distinct, modern tone and sometimes sings along with his solos via a lapel mic. He attended Berklee College of Music (though he left before graduating to tour with Gary Burton). He has worked with Larry Goldings, Joe Henderson, Mark Turner, and was the first guitarist in Brian Blade’s Fellowship Band.

    In addition to original compositions, Rosenwinkel also formed a Standards Trio around 2009, focusing on classic tunes. His approach to standards is both respectful of tradition and uniquely modern.

- heading: Lionel Loueke
  artists:
  - name: Lionel Loueke
    born: 1973
  prose: |-
    Lionel Loueke is from Benin, West Africa. He began guitar at age 17 and eventually studied at Berklee and the Monk Institute. He was voted top Rising Star guitarist in DownBeat multiple times.

    Loueke has played with Herbie Hancock, contributing to the Grammy-winning *River: The Joni Letters* . He often incorporates African rhythmic and melodic elements, including tongue clicks inspired by South African languages.

- heading: Mary Halvorson
  artists:
  - name: Mary Halvorson
    born: 1980
  prose: |-
    Mary Halvorson is a guitarist and composer. She won DownBeat’s best guitarist several years running and a 2019 MacArthur fellowship. She teaches at The New School. Her style is often avant-garde, blending experimental rock, folk, and indie influences with jazz. She’s known for pitch-bending electronics and balancing delicate lines with "sprays of atmospheric noise."

- heading: Julian Lage
  artists:
  - name: Julian Lage
    born: 1987
  prose: |-
    Julian Lage was a child prodigy. The documentary *Jules at Eight* featured him as a child. He studied at various institutions including Berklee. His first album, *Sounding Point* , was nominated for a Grammy in 2010 (Best Contemporary Jazz Album).

    Lage’s music often blends jazz with Americana and bluegrass influences. On “The Informant,” he collaborates with Bela Fleck (banjo) and Chris Thile (mandolin), both notable for their jazz/bluegrass fusion.

    ### Béla Fleck (1958-)

    Béla Fleck is a banjo player known for his group The Flecktones, a jazz/bluegrass fusion band.

    ### Chris Thile (1981-)

    Chris Thile is a mandolinist, also a former child prodigy, member of Nickel Creek, and host of NPR’s *Live From Here* .

- heading: Brian Blade
  artists:
  - name: Brian Blade
    born: 1970
  prose: |-
    Brian Blade is a composer and one of the most sought-after drummers in jazz. He studied at Loyola University in New Orleans and has worked with a who’s who of jazz, including Joshua Redman, Christian McBride, Chick Corea, Pat Metheny, and Wayne Shorter.

    In 1998, with pianist Jon Cowherd, he formed the Fellowship Band (which once included Kurt Rosenwinkel). The group’s sound blends jazz with pygmy chants, American folk, and spiritual elements. Their album *Landmarks* (2014) exemplifies this holistic style.
