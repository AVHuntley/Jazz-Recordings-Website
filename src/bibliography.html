Survey of Jazz Recordings

A history of jazz styles and figures through selected recordings

Bibliography

These are the principal sources consulted in creating this survey. Citations throughout the articles—which look like (Sultanof, p.13) or (Bierman, p.103)—link back to the corresponding entry on this page. Where a source is freely available online, the entry includes a link.
